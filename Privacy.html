Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zickk ("Zickk", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") approaches privacy when you use the platform.

Privacy first.
Zickk is designed to respect users' privacy and to collect and retain only what is reasonably necessary for the operation and display of the service.

1. Privacy and Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, publish it publicly, or provide it to third parties.

Zickk does not use users' personal information for sale, advertising resale, or public distribution.

2. Information and Content

Zickk is primarily a publishing platform. Content you choose to upload or publish is handled only as necessary to provide the features you have chosen to use and to display the content according to your settings and actions.

We do not retain uploaded content for purposes unrelated to providing the service. Content that you permit to be displayed may be stored and processed as necessary to make that content available through Zickk.

You remain responsible for the content and information you choose to publish or make available.

3. App Permissions

Zickk requests only the permissions needed for applicable upload and media functionality.

Permission requests are used to allow you to select or provide content for upload. Zickk does not request unnecessary device permissions for unrelated purposes.

4. Identity and Privacy

We aim to protect user identity and privacy as much as reasonably possible through the design and operation of the platform.

We do not intentionally expose users' personal information to other parties. Users should nevertheless avoid publishing or sharing information they do not want to become visible to other people.

5. Account Security

Users are responsible for protecting their accounts, devices, passwords, authentication credentials, and other information that could be used to access their accounts.

You should never disclose your password or authentication credentials to another person or expose them in a way that could result in account loss or unauthorized access.

Any loss, compromise, leak, or exposure caused by the user's own actions, device, credentials, or failure to protect their account remains the user's responsibility.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect the platform and information processed through it.

However, no online service, device, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users acknowledge that no system can eliminate every possible security risk.

7. Account Deletion

When you delete your account, Zickk will process the deletion of information associated with that account according to the applicable deletion process.

Zickk does not intend to retain personal information associated with a deleted account after deletion has been completed, except where temporary technical processing is necessary to complete the deletion.

Zickk does not provide deleted-account personal information to third parties.

8. Third-Party Services

Zickk may use third-party technical services for functions such as authentication, hosting, databases, storage, content delivery, or infrastructure.

Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide the technical services used by Zickk. Zickk does not sell users' personal information to those providers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the Last updated date will be updated on this page.
